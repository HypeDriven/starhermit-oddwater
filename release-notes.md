# 1.1 — Living water remaster

- Four distinct FLUX → TRELLIS fish models, with textured scales, flexible body and tail animation, and four levels of detail.
- StarHermit Engine GPU FFT water, focused caustics, planar reflections, refraction, light shafts and Fresnel-shaded air bubbles.
- Thickness-based subsurface and transmitted-light shading on fish and aquatic leaves.
- A voxel collision grid for 3,000 settling pebbles. Water changes and clicking the substrate disturb the gravel.
- Cherry and Amano shrimp, nerite and ramshorn snails, mayfly nymphs and a water boatman, with individual movement and inspection cards.
- MOSS aquarium ambience and action sounds, plus a 90-second YuE instrumental soundtrack.

Existing aquarium saves continue to work. Open Options and choose Ultra to see the richest water and fish detail.
