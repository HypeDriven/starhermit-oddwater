# Oddwater

A living freshwater aquarium with a slightly impossible imagination. Tend a planted community tank, get to know its residents, and awaken three surreal dreams.

Watch neon tetras and harlequin rasboras school through the plants, peppered corys forage over the gravel, and a pearl gourami patrol the water. Shrimp browse the substrate, snails graze the glass, and tiny freshwater insects explore the shallows.

Open the **keeper’s notebook** to choose foods, improve filtration and aeration, adjust the light cycle, grow plants from cuttings, and raise juveniles in a sheltered nursery. Name your fish, build their trust, complete the field guide, and work through ongoing keeper projects. Your aquarium’s history and progress save automatically.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and click a resident to inspect it. On phones, use the zoom buttons, Notebook and Tank notes. Press **J** for the notebook or use the gear button for graphics, sound, controls and accessibility.

Choose **Ultra** for the richest water, reflections, caustics and fish textures. Turn Sound on for soft aquarium ambience and an original instrumental soundtrack.
